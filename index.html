MY STORY

How my perspective has changed

Moving to the U.S. and returning to Brazil each summer

Going back home didn’t change the opportunities in front of me, it changed how I saw them.

The first summer I went back to Brazil after starting college, I saw home differently. I had always known I was lucky to study and live in the United States, but during that visit, I started to really see what that meant. Being able to get an education and have choices about my future was a blessing I didn’t want to take for granted.

When I came back to the U.S., I pushed myself harder. College was more personal because I understood how much the opportunity mattered to me. I wanted my effort to reflect my gratitude.

That summer changed the way I think about growth. For me, it started with recognizing what I already had and deciding to do more with it. I’m still figuring out where my education will take me, but I want to look back knowing I gave myself the chance to find out.
